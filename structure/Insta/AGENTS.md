# Instagram post direction

- All new posts use a white theme: Off-White `#F5F3EF`, Night `#0E0E10` text, and restrained Burnt Orange `#E35205` accents.
- Put the official logo at the top-left of every slide. Use the existing logo asset; preserve its proportions.
- Generate only reusable background artwork with image generation. Add titles, photos, names, roles, numbering, and other variable content through a Python renderer.
- The reusable team carousel renderer is `team-members/render.py`, with content in `team-members/content.json` and current exports in `team-members/white/`.
